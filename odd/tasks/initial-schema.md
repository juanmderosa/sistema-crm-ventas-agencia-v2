## Specs

- S1 Roles are exactly `owner`, `admin`, `seller`; no other staff role exists in the MVP.
- S2 Every staff account has exactly one profile row, keyed by its `auth.users` id, with a display name and an active flag.
- S3 Authorization is enforced by the database: a seller can only read its own profile, while Owner/Admin can read every profile.
- S4 A seller cannot create, update or delete profiles through the API; only Owner/Admin may mutate staff profiles.
- S5 The last active Owner can never be demoted or deactivated, including concurrent attempts.
- S6 No public registration path exists: `anon` and `authenticated` have no insert policy on staff profiles.
- S7 The customer-facing schema never exposes internal prices or draft vehicles to anonymous callers (applies to the inventory slice).
- S8 An inquiry keeps one responsible person and can record several vehicle interests without creating extra inquiries or reservations (inquiry slice).
- S9 A vehicle can hold at most one active reservation and an inquiry can hold at most one active reservation (reservation slice).
- S10 Only an explicit Owner/Admin confirmation marks a vehicle Sold and counts a sale (sale slice).
- S10a Confirmed T5 boundary: "Si, confirmo ese límite. Revisemos engram." Confirmed-sale reversal is excluded; reopening resumes follow-up without changing Sold inventory or confirmed-sale history.
- S10b Pending-review reopening: "Confirmo esa regla." Require Owner/Admin confirmation or rejection of a pending sale review before reopening its inquiry.
- S10c Inquiry-linked sales only: "Si, hagamoslo así." Every sale requires an inquiry and explicit Owner/Admin confirmation; block direct inventory creation or updates introducing Sold without that confirmation, including manually registered sales.
- S10d Blocked-sale eligibility: "Si, debe impedir hasta que se desbloquee." Both reporting and confirming a sale require the vehicle to be enabled for sale; reject until explicitly unblocked.
- S11 Approved phone criterion: "Si, usemos ese criterio" — international phone input with a country selector defaulting to Argentina; persist normalized numbers without guessing ambiguous prefixes. Phone matching is not authentication.
- S12 Approved rotation criterion: "Usemos esa regla" — round robin per originating vehicle among currently eligible participants; continuity does not advance rotation.
- S13 Approved timestamp tie-break: "lo aplicamos" — equal creation timestamps are resolved by sequential creation order, latest first.
- S14 Delivery: "Prefiero que se unan primero en feat/inquiries" — accumulate reviewable deliveries on `feat/inquiries` before integration into `develop`.
- S15 Contact-edit protection: "Si, usemos esa protección contra sobreescrituras." Reject stale edits without persisting any change and explicitly request reloading the contact. Preserve role restrictions and submitted inquiry snapshots.
- S16 Implementation authorization: "Bien, implementemos si está todo listo para eso." T3c covers controlled Available-interest additions, authorized name/email edits and Owner/Admin-only normalized-phone corrections with collision rejection; no generic table mutations or status/reassignment APIs.
- S17 Unified CRM: "Si, me parece mejor. Incluso, que dueños o admins puedan hacer el mismo flujo." Manual inquiries support in-person and telephone origins for active Seller/Owner/Admin. Every commercial reservation requires an inquiry and contact; preserve web origin and existing web submission behavior.
- S18 Manual assignment: "Si, quien la registra". Assign manual inquiries to their registering staff member without invoking or advancing web rotation.
- S19 Manual minimum data: "Me parece bien." Approves mandatory name and phone with optional email for manual entry; web email remains mandatory. Reuse existing contacts without automatic overwrites or unauthorized history access; allow new contacts.
- S19a Manual inquiry without a vehicle: "Si, me parece bien" — a manual inquiry may be created without selecting an originating or interest vehicle; reserving still requires selecting an Available vehicle listed as an interest of that inquiry.
- S20 Internal blocking: "La única diferencia es que dueños o admins pueden quitar un vehículo de la venta (habilitarlo o deshabilitarlo, es decir bloquear el vehículo)." Reservation and block are separate actions. "Si, bloquiemos eso." Reject blocking a vehicle while it has an active reservation, without implicit cancellation. "Correcto, no debe mostrarse en el sitio." Blocked inventory is not public; private authorized information and history remain available.
- S21 Statistics definitions approved 2026-11-04: "Si, cuando la ficha pública se carga correctamente." — a view is the successful load of the public vehicle-detail page, not catalog appearance; only the public site emits views, the private panel never. "Si, estoy de acuerdo con la venta de 24hs móviles." — rolling 24-hour dedup per vehicle per opaque random browser token with no personal data. "El panel privado no requiere generación de métricas por vistas, eso es solo para el sitio público." and "No quiero verificiar identidad de visitantes" — no identity verification of any kind. "Si" — server-side filter for known bot User-Agents, accepted as imperfect MVP scope. "Está perfecta" — fixed `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires` attribution with local-midnight cuts for month/quarter/semester/year and inclusive custom ranges. "Si" — activities stay with their performer; inquiry outcome attributed to the responsible person at recording time. "Si, está bien" — same-period web-only conversion relations labeled as relation, never causality; manual/in-person/phone counts shown apart. "Si" — seller sees only own figures, never totals, others' figures, comparisons or per-vehicle views. "si" — visit requests counted as their own event; results/cancellations deferred to visit-flow design. "si" — sale counted once at Owner/Admin confirmation; no reversals in the MVP. Full definitions: `docs/product-plan.md` §7 and `docs/decisions.md`.

- S21a Visit metric delivery amendment: "Diferir al flujo de visitas (Recomendado)" — preserve the approved visit-request counting definition, but implement it with the visit-flow feature when its event source exists, not in T6.
- S21b Repeated closure counting: "si" — user confirms counting each closed-without-sale recording as a separate event attributed to the responsible person at that recording; reopening preserves earlier outcomes and does not duplicate inquiries. Confirmed sales remain counted once.

- S21c Historical closure attribution: "si" — user confirms unknown responsibility for historical closures without reliable snapshots and prospective responsibility capture for new closures, with no fabricated backfill.
- S21d Inquiry responsibility counts: "Si, lo confirmo" — count an inquiry once per distinct responsible person in their first-assignment period. Ana January → Bruno February → Ana March counts Ana January once and Bruno February once, not Ana March again. Global remains one; staff counts are nonadditive.
- S21e Incomplete inquiry history: "Si" — keep inquiries with incomplete responsibility history in global counts and a separate incomplete-history bucket, without assigning them to per-person periods using unprovable dates. Reliable sale/closure attribution remains unaffected.
