## Specs

- S1 — User authorization: "Si, comencemos la implementación." In context, begin the first proposed block: "horarios, estructura de visitas, historial y permisos". Approved product requirements are in `docs/product-plan.md` sections 6–7 and `docs/decisions.md` S21a tables. Public form and internal agenda are deferred; no deployment or remote work.
- S2 — Approved configuration: "Dueño/Admin configura días e intervalos de atención, sin duración fija ni cupos." Only active Owner/Admin may change opening hours; staff may read them. Validate weekday and ordered, nonempty same-day intervals. No invented default business hours. Changes preserve existing visits.
- S3 — Approved cardinality: "En principio dejemos una, y la posibilidad de crear nuevas." At most one Requested/Confirmed visit per inquiry, not per vehicle. Each visit references a concrete interest vehicle. Keep completed visits and allow later visits; optional free-text purpose. Foundation introduces the private storage and constraints, not creation or lifecycle RPCs.
- S4 — Approved permissions: current responsible seller sees their inquiry visits and history; active Owner/Admin sees all. Former assignees, inactive users, anonymous callers and unrelated sellers must not gain detail access. Mutations go through controlled operations, not direct application-role table writes. Keep attribution distinct from recording actors, including first-result unassigned versus never recorded.
- S5 — Approved history: "Si se cargó un resultado erroneo, el vendedor también puede cambiarla. Es decir, puede editar el estaod." Preserve old/new state, actor, timestamp and correction reason. Later lifecycle work supplies operations enforcing the approved transition/correction rules; no lifecycle completeness claim for the foundation.
- S6 — T2 authorization: "Vamos al siguiente bloque". Implement "creación de visitas desde una consulta existente y solicitud pública con reparto atómico". Staff creation reuses an active inquiry and an existing vehicle interest; no new inquiry, reservation or routing change. Staff must be active, currently responsible or Owner/Admin. Private unpublished and Reserved vehicles are allowed; Sold, deleted and blocked vehicles are not.
- S7 — Public visit requests create inquiry and visit together through the existing submission/routing operation, preserving canonical contact fields, continuity, rotation and accepted-only privacy-safe retry responses. Retain original visit intent for matching retries; changed input or ordinary-inquiry versus visit-request ID reuse is rejected. Failure rolls back contact, inquiry, routing and visit effects together. New visits require future local time within configured opening intervals; retries do not become new visits or depend on later schedule/vehicle changes.
- S8 — T3 authorization: "vamos" after the proposed next block "T3, gestión de estados y reprogramaciones". Current responsible active staff or Owner/Admin may confirm, cancel, record results and correct final states. User approved late initial confirmation ("si"). Normal attendance/no-show requires Confirmed and reached appointment time. Requested/Confirmed may cancel. No automatic transitions, cross-flow cancellation, reporting or UI in T3.
- S9 — Historical corrections: user approved ("si") final-to-final corrections despite closed inquiry or Sold/blocked vehicle, with mandatory nonblank reason and history. Returning to pending requires active inquiry, eligible vehicle and no other pending visit. Past Confirmed restoration is allowed. Preserve original request intent and first-outcome timestamp/responsibility, including an explicitly unassigned first outcome, through correction/reactivation/refinalization. Record old/new state, appointment, actor, reason and time atomically. Same-state authorized calls are documented no-ops, not new corrections.
- S10 — Reschedule pending visits only, without a quantity limit, to a future date/time within current opening hours. Explicit agreement selects Confirmed, a proposal selects Requested. Preserve visit identity, purpose, attribution and original creation intent; append old/new appointment and state. Reuse the existing post-lock opening-time helper. Opening-hour changes do not invalidate an existing appointment during state-only confirmation/correction.

- S11 — T4 authorization: "Revisemos y cerremos T3b Y luego pasemos a T4". Inquiry closure cancels only Requested/Confirmed visits with "Consulta cerrada"; completed visits remain unchanged. User clarification: "Si, venta concretada cancela inmediatamente la visita pendiente." Reporting a sale cancels that inquiry's pending visit immediately, even for a different interest vehicle; other inquiries remain unchanged until their vehicle is sold, blocked or soft-deleted.
- S12 — Vehicle Sold, first soft deletion and internal blocking cancel only that vehicle's pending visits, with "Vehículo vendido", "Vehículo dado de baja" and "Vehículo bloqueado" respectively. Reserved keeps pending visits. Reopening/unblocking does not restore visits. Preserve first-outcome attribution (including unassigned), immutable creation intent, appointment and purpose; record actor, reason and old/new state atomically. Existing authorization and commercial operation results remain unchanged except these approved cancellation effects. T5 reporting and UI remain excluded.
